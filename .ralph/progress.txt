## Codebase Patterns
- Docker build: `backend/Dockerfile` — 2-stage (builder compiles .py→.pyc then deletes .py, runtime copies result)
- `.dockerignore` at `backend/.dockerignore` — excludes scripts/, tests/ from build context (seeds now included)
- The find-delete command excludes migrations/ AND seeds/: `find /app -type f -name "*.py" ! -path "/app/migrations/*" ! -path "/app/seeds/*" -delete`
- Seeds at `backend/seeds/` — memory_seeder.py, user_seeder.py
- Build script: `backend/scripts/build.sh`
- Registry: `ghcr.io/ruska-ai/orchestra`
