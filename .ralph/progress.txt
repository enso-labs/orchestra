## Codebase Patterns
- Skills live in `.claude/skills/<name>/SKILL.md` with YAML frontmatter (name + description)
- Trigger keywords go in the YAML `description` field, not in the markdown body
- Skills must be under 5,000 words
- Use imperative form for all instructions (Analyze, Decompose, Spawn, not "You should...")
- No typecheck/lint needed for markdown-only changes in `.claude/` directory
